# Week 6 · Capstone, demo and careers

## The idea in one line

Narrow a question until data can answer it, answer it with evidence, and show one real thing.

## Capstone tiers

- No code: a report or dashboard, with a date and a source on every number.
- Light code: a TypeScript tool (Pyth price, Jupiter quote, health checker).
- On-chain: a tested Anchor program deployed to devnet.

## Four lenses on any protocol

1. Financial: how does it earn, and who pays users?
2. Technical: which programs, accounts and transactions?
3. Risk: what could make users lose money?
4. Strategic: who uses it, and who competes?
